Players open the game page and log in with their college id. The Game Master opens the same page and chooses “Game Master”. The two judges choose “Judge” and enter their own passcode (Judge 1: 24681357, Judge 2: 13572468). After each round they rate every layout from 0 to 5 in steps of 0.5; those marks make up the 10 points for layout and flow.
