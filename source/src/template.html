    Rules at a glance
    • Line order: RM Processor → Assembler → Robo Welder → Packer. A machine only works if it is fed by the stage before it, by touching it or by a complete belt.
    • An RM Processor must touch the inbound bay, or take a belt from it (first motor touches the bay, second motor touches the RM Processor). A Packer must touch the outbound bay, or send a belt to it (first motor touches the Packer, second motor touches the bay).
    • The line rate is the lowest stage capacity, so extra machines in a strong stage earn nothing.
    • Belt: motor (blue → green), connectors, motor. Each motor must touch a machine or a docking bay, two different ones in all. The first motor touches the machine upstream, the second touches the one downstream. A complete belt gives +10% to every machine touching a motor (max +30%).
    • Two belts may not touch, corners included. Keep one empty cell between them.
    • To lay connectors quickly, tap the Connector card, then drag across the floor. One Undo removes the whole line. While it is on, pan with a right-drag or two fingers; tap the card again to stop.
    • Storage units hold any material. Each should touch the IN or OUT bay, or touch another unit that does.
    • Nothing can be built on the docking bays or the grey obstacles. Keep an open aisle from IN to OUT. Spending is capped by the budget.
