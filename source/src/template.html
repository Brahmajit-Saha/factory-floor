Highlights

Production line

Capacity is what the fed machines could make. Running at is what really flows through them, because each piece splits its output evenly between the places it feeds. The line rate is what reaches the outbound bay. ◂ marks the stage that is the bottleneck.

Layout and flow (marked by the judges)

Two judges each give up to 5 points after the round. These checks show what to look for. They are not scored by the computer.

    Numbers

    Truck run

    Storage and the line start empty. A delivery truck arrives every 60 seconds (the game master can change this) and a shipping truck collects finished goods one interval later. What your storage holds follows the round clock, and your score uses the whole round.

    Deliveries and space needed

    Rules at a glance
    • Line order: RM Processor → Assembler → Robo Welder → Packer. A machine only works if it is fed by the stage before it, by touching it or by a complete belt.
    • An RM Processor must touch the inbound bay (or a storage unit fed from it), or take a belt from it. A Packer must touch the outbound bay (or a storage unit that leads to it), or send a belt there.
    • Flow splits evenly: a piece that feeds several places shares its output between them, and a machine makes the lesser of what it receives and its own rate. The line rate is what reaches the outbound bay.
    • Belt: motor, connectors, motor. Material enters a motor at its blue end and leaves at its green end (the arrow shows the way). Each motor must touch a machine or a docking bay, two different ones in all. The first motor touches the machine upstream, the second touches the one downstream. A complete belt gives +10% to every machine touching a motor (max +30%).
    • Two belts may not touch, corners included. Keep one empty cell between them.
    • Limits: a machine takes at most 2 belts in and 2 out, a storage unit 3 in and 3 out (joined storage units add up). Extra belts are ignored and outlined in red.
    • To lay connectors quickly, tap the Connector card, then drag across the floor. One Undo removes the whole line. While it is on, pan with a right-drag or two fingers; tap the card again to stop.
    • Storage starts empty. A delivery truck arrives every 60 seconds and a shipping truck collects finished goods one interval later; the game master can change the interval or send extra trucks. Put storage between the inbound bay and the RM Processor, and between the Packer and the outbound bay, touching or joined by a complete belt. Arrows show the flow. Full storage turns material away.
    • Nothing can be built on the docking bays or the grey obstacles. Keep an open aisle from IN to OUT. Spending is capped by the budget.
